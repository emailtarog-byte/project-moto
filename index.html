Recommendations

Top 5 JDM Cars

Iconic Japanese performance cars—looking to start a project build or invest in genuine automotive history? Explore our definitive countdown of golden-era Japanese performance icons.

  1. #1 Nissan Skyline GT-R (R34)

    Engine: 2.6L Twin-Turbo Inline-6 (RB26DETT)

    Drivetrain: ATTESA E-TS All-Wheel Drive with Super-HICAS

    Why Pick It: Racing pedigree, aggressive styling, advanced AWD traction, and iconic twin-turbo soundtrack.

  2. #2 Toyota Supra MK4

    Engine: 3.0L Twin-Turbo Inline-6 (2JZ-GTE)

    Drivetrain: Rear-Wheel Drive (6-Speed Manual)

    Why Pick It: Legendary iron-block reliability, massive aftermarket power potential, and iconic styling.

  3. #3 Mazda RX-7 (FD)

    Engine: 1.3L Twin-Turbo Rotary (13B-REW)

    Drivetrain: Rear-Wheel Drive

    Why Pick It: Near-perfect 50:50 weight balance, screaming high-revving rotary engine, and timeless design.

  4. #4 Honda NSX (NA1)

    Engine: 3.0L Naturally Aspirated V6 VTEC (C30A)

    Drivetrain: Mid-Engine, Rear-Wheel Drive

    Why Pick It: Ayrton Senna-tuned handling dynamics, lightweight all-aluminum chassis, and pure VTEC throttle response.

  5. #5 Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution VI

    Engine: 2.0L Turbo Inline-4 (4G63T)

    Drivetrain: All-Wheel Drive with Active Yaw Control

    Why Pick It: Championship rally heritage, instant turbo response, and razor-sharp AWD grip.
